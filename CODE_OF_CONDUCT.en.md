# Aletheia Nexus (溯光) Code of Conduct

[中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.en.md)

Researchers, developers and people new to research software should be able to ask questions, discuss evidence, identify problems and contribute safely. Technical disagreement is welcome; participants deserve respect.

## Shared expectations

- Discuss problems, evidence and implementations without personal attacks.
- Respect different experience levels, languages and backgrounds. Help newcomers and remain open to corrections.
- Report experiments and validation honestly, distinguish fact, inference and uncertainty, and credit contributions and sources appropriately.
- Do not harass, threaten, insult, discriminate, persistently provoke in bad faith or disclose another person's identity, accounts, contact details or private information without permission.
- Do not demand API keys, institutional credentials or research materials that someone is not entitled to disclose as a condition of participation.

## Scope

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation collaboration and community activities representing the project. Maintainers follow the same standards; technical seniority or role does not provide an exception.

## Reporting and response

You may leave a discussion and report inappropriate behavior through a private contact channel publicly provided by the maintainers. If none is available, open an issue requesting private contact without posting incident details. Do not disclose affected people's information in public. Use the [security reporting instructions](SECURITY.en.md) for vulnerabilities.

Maintainers assess the facts, impact and repeated behavior. Responses may include clarification or mediation, a request to stop, editing or removing inappropriate content, or temporary or ongoing participation restrictions. Protect reporters' and affected people's privacy where possible and explain decisions within an appropriate scope. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is unacceptable.

This code expresses expectations for participation. It does not promise a specific response time or require anyone to disclose personal experiences publicly.
